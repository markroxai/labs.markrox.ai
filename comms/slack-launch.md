:test_tube: **Introducing AI Eval Labs**

I'm starting a weekly series of hands-on builds focused on **AI evaluation**: how we know whether a model or agent is actually good, and whether a change made it better or worse.

Each week is one lab, and each lab ships:
• **Code**: open repo, runnable, with tests
• **Demo**: a short recording of it working
• **Write-up**: what I built and what I learned

**First up:**
• Lab 00: Setup and a reference agent everything else plugs into
• Lab 01: Trajectory grading (pinpointing the exact step where an agent run broke)
• Lab 02: Statistical significance (is that 2% improvement real or noise?)
• Lab 03: Calibrating LLM-as-a-judge against human labels

Later labs cover RAG robustness, agent red-teaming, CI regression gates, drift monitoring, and cost/quality tradeoffs.

:link: Progress and links: https://labs.markrox.ai
:point_right: **Want to join in?** Every lab has a core scope you can finish in a day. Pick one, try it, and post what you find here. Questions, critiques, and "we tried this on project X" stories are all welcome.

All data is synthetic or public. No client data.
